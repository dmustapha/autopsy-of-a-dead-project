## THE SIGNATURE — "the flatline" (an OBJECT/mechanism, not a chart)
A forensic **vital-signs trace** of the repo's commit pulse. It is a MECHANISM that performs the
core verb (diagnose how it died), encodes every state, and is sketchable from memory.
- **What it does:** each commit is a beat; the trace's amplitude + rhythm = commit cadence; as the
  repo dies the beats thin and shrink (cadence decay), then the line goes FLAT at the last commit.
  The flat tail length = days since last commit. At the verdict moment it flatlines and stamps.
- **Encodes all states:** `alive` = still beating (green); `abandoned` = flat, long flat tail;
  `burned_out` = erratic beats then a sudden drop; `pivoted` = beats resume elsewhere (a gap then a
  faint second rhythm); `completed` = a clean, deliberate stop (a final full beat then flat). Engine
  state + confidence annotate the trace, never fabricated.
- **A fresh critic must call it** "the repo's last heartbeat / a flatlining monitor," NOT "a line
  chart." The mechanism (beats → decay → flat → stamp) is the tell, not the line.
- **Everywhere, VARIED in role/scale (anti motif-monotony, OWNER-VERDICT amp lesson):**
  - logo/favicon: a single pulse mark with a flat tail (the glyph).
  - hero: the full trace running live, then flattening ≤2s after view → the money moment.
  - certificate (hero artifact): the frozen flatline strip across the header, time-of-death marked.
  - evidence section: NOT the flatline again — the 6 features as discrete vital-sign readouts.
  - dividers: a thin flat rule with one residual blip (restraint, not the full motif).
  - dead/empty/error states: a flat line, no beat (dignified absence).
- **Must MOVE** (coldspot lesson): verify with getAnimations(); frame-0 paints current state; rAF
  animates the beat + the flatten; honest final value (no rolling wrong numbers).
