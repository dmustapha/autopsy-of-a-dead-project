## World Statement
A coroner's bench in a dark, warm-lit morgue for dead code — a dignified autopsy and remembrance
(Día de los Muertos: memorial, never mockery) where a repository's death is certified honestly,
with the evidence shown and nothing faked.

## Accent Color
`#D4A017` (autopsy-amber; hi `#E8B733`, deep `#6b5210`). Single accent. Alive-state signal is
toe-tag green `#8FB339`. Ground is warm near-black `#0c0b09`.

## Background Treatment
Constructed field — warm near-black ground + a faint graph-paper grid (≤5% alpha) + a single
breathing amber "lamp" radial glow + sparse drifting dust + vignette. Never a flat #000 panel;
exactly one colored ambient glow; elevation via bg-lightness step + hairline, never black shadow.
(specimen-under-glass atmosphere, axon/leash register).

## Typography Domain
Forensic / memorial (dark-forensic register — axon's lane). Pairing, three fonts three jobs:
IBM Plex Mono (evidence data + uppercase tracked eyebrows) · Spectral serif (the coroner's eulogy
prose) · serif display for the certificate title + confidence numeral. Evidence numerals tabular,
right-aligned.

## Rejection List
No flat #000 panels; no black drop-shadows (glow-only elevation); no hex-zoo (≤6 role hues, shades
are alphas of a role); no neon confidence/verdict; the signature object must NOT read as a
chart/gauge/health-bar; no dev-jargon in card copy; no fabricated posterior when the engine is on
its labeled fallback (show a dignified "single determination — no posterior" note instead); theme
must be memorial/reverent, never gory/mocking.
